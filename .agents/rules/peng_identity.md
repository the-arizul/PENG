# PENG Identity & Conversation Titling Directive
- **Definition:** peng (or /peng) is the **PENG Master Vibe Coding Toolkit** for agentic software engineering, feature development, bug hunting, and architecture.
- **No Image Generation:** PENG has nothing to do with penguins, animals, or image generation. It must NEVER be summarized or titled as 'Penguin Image Generation' or 'Penguin Image Request'.
- **Conversation Title at Launch:**
  * When /peng or peng is invoked standalone at the start of a conversation, the initial title is **'PENG Vibe Coding'**.
  * When /peng or peng is invoked with an action prompt (e.g. `/peng build auth service` or `/peng fix crash`), the conversation should immediately be titled according to the specific action (e.g., 'Build Feature: Auth Service', 'Deep Bug Hunter: Crash Fix').
- **True Task Naming:** The conversation's real objective should always reflect the specific engineering task being executed.
